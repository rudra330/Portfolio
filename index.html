It is designed to help users improve their English-speaking skills, fluency, and confidence through regular practice. 📁 Hand rehab(Robotics) project 2 Files

Personal Hand Rehabilitation Assistant

Developed a portable, home-based bilateral smart glove system for post-stroke hand rehabilitation that integrates mirror therapy with real-time sensor motion tracking and motor-assisted actuation. The healthy-hand transmitter glove uses a calibrated flex sensor on the index finger to measure bending angles and a palmar force-sensitive resistor (FSR) to detect grip force, processed by an ESP32 microcontroller and transmitted wirelessly with minimal latency. On the paralyzed-hand receiver glove, a second ESP32 and a PCA9685 motor driver control five MG996R servo motors on a custom PCB/Veroboard circuit—proportionally replicating finger flexion and triggering synchronized multi-finger grasping based on palmar grip pressure while mitigating power fluctuations and servo jitter.

IEEE Conference Manuscript · 2026 First-Listed Co-Author (†Equal Contrib.)

"A Hybrid MDM-LDA Framework for Robust Motor Imagery Classification in Stroke Patients"

R. Chakraborty†, M.Z.I. Mahi†, M.Z. Hasan†, and M.F.K. Khondakar (Dept. of BME, CUET)

  • Analyzed public 50-subject stroke patient EEG dataset across 7 temporal windows (2–6 s) and 19 frequency sub-bands (8–30 Hz).
  • Extracted 449 spatial covariance (Riemannian tangent-space) and inter-hemispheric coherence features with adaptive trial-level MDM + LDA selection.
  • Achieved 82.0% mean accuracy (Cohen's κ = 0.62) and balanced right/left hand F1-score of 81.14%, outperforming prior benchmark (72.21%).
  • Applied explainable AI (XAI averaged LDA weights) to rank top discriminative neuro-rehabilitation channels.
Submitted to IEEE QPAIN / ICCIT 2026
